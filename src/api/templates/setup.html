🔌 Connect to TikTok — Step by Step

{# ── STEP 1: Create Developer Account ───────────────────── #}
Step 1 Create a TikTok Developer account
  1. Go to developers.tiktok.com
  2. Click "Log in" in the top-right — use the same TikTok account you want to post from
  3. Click your avatar → "Manage apps"
  4. Click "Connect an app" then "Create an app"

{# ── STEP 3: Add Content Posting API ────────────────────── #}
Step 3 Add the "Content Posting API" product to your app
  1. Inside your new app on the developer portal, find the "Add products" section
  2. Click "Content Posting API" → Add it
  3. Under Scopes, enable: video.publish and video.upload
  4. Click "Submit for review" — TikTok approves in 1–3 business days
While waiting for approval, your app works in Sandbox mode — it will post to a test account rather than your real TikTok. You can still generate tokens and test everything.
{# ── STEP 4: Get your token ──────────────────────────────── #}
Step 4 Get your Access Token
  1. In your app on the developer portal, go to "Tools & Resources" → "API Explorer"
  2. Select your app from the dropdown
  3. Select the scope video.publish
  4. Click "Get Token" — it will open TikTok for you to authorise
  5. After authorising, copy the Access Token and Open ID shown on screen
Tokens expire after 24 hours in Sandbox mode, and after 30–90 days in production. You'll need to repeat this step when it expires.
